Privacy and transparency

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

ACSKTech (“ACSKTech”, “we”, “us” or “our”) respects your privacy. This notice explains how we handle personal data when you visit acsktech.in or submit an enquiry or feedback. It is intended to describe our practices in line with applicable Indian data-protection and information-technology laws, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and rules to the extent in force and applicable. Legal commencement and obligations may be phased; this notice is not a legal opinion or a guarantee of compliance.

1. Information we collect

When you use our forms, we may receive your name, email address, selected service or feedback rating, and the message you choose to send. Our hosting and anti-abuse services may process technical information such as IP address, browser/device details, timestamps and verification signals to deliver the site, prevent spam and protect our forms. Please do not submit passwords, payment-card information, government identification numbers, sensitive personal information, or confidential client/customer records through these forms.

2. Purposes and use

We use form information to respond to your enquiry, review feedback, communicate about your request, protect the website from abuse, and maintain necessary business or security records. We do not use these forms to create a website user account. We do not intentionally store form submissions in a website database; messages are sent to our business inbox.

3. Processing and service providers

The website is hosted using Cloudflare Pages and its Pages Functions. We plan to use Cloudflare Turnstile to help distinguish legitimate form submissions from automated abuse. Form messages are delivered using Resend to our business inbox at acsktech@gmail.com. These providers may process information needed to provide, secure and troubleshoot their services, under their own terms and privacy notices. Depending on their systems, processing may occur outside India. We do not sell personal data.

Turnstile must be enabled and its server-side verification configured before we describe it as active on the live forms. Cloudflare's own privacy terms also apply to its processing.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

We do not intentionally use these forms for advertising profiles. Cloudflare or other infrastructure providers may use technical signals, cookies or similar technologies for security, performance and abuse prevention. Your browser settings may let you manage cookies, although disabling essential security features may affect site functionality.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We disclose information to the service providers described above only as reasonably needed to operate the website, verify submissions and deliver messages, or where disclosure is required by law, a lawful request, or to protect rights and security. We do not disclose it for unrelated marketing by third parties.

6. Retention

We retain messages in our business email and related records only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, follow up, maintain legitimate business records, resolve disputes, protect against abuse, or meet legal obligations. Provider logs and backups may follow their providers' retention periods. We may retain limited records where legally necessary even after a deletion request.

7. Your requests and choices

You may choose not to submit a form. You can contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you submitted, or to raise a privacy concern. We will consider and respond to requests in accordance with applicable law, after taking reasonable steps to verify the request where appropriate. Some information may need to be retained for legal, security or legitimate recordkeeping reasons.

8. Children

Our business enquiry and feedback forms are intended for business and general website communication, not for collecting children's data. Please do not submit personal information about a child through these forms unless legally authorised and necessary.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures appropriate to the nature of these forms, including server-side handling of email credentials and request validation. No website, transmission method or provider can guarantee absolute security. Please contact us promptly if you believe information you submitted has been exposed or misused.

10. Contact and privacy grievances

For privacy questions, requests or complaints, email acsktech@gmail.com with the subject “Privacy Request”. Please describe your request without including unnecessary sensitive information. We will acknowledge and handle it within the period required by applicable law. ACSKTech can be contacted through this email address.

11. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when our practices, service providers or legal requirements change. The revision date at the top indicates the latest update.

Important: This notice is a practical website privacy draft, not a substitute for advice from an Indian privacy lawyer. Before relying on it as a compliance statement, confirm the applicability and commencement of relevant provisions, the exact data flows and provider terms, and whether additional business-specific disclosures or grievance details are required.
