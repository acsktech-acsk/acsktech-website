Privacy and transparency

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Information you provide

If you use our contact or feedback forms, we receive the details you submit, such as your name, email address, selected service or rating, and message. Please do not submit passwords, payment-card details, sensitive personal information, or confidential client records through these forms.

How we use it

We use submitted information to respond to enquiries, review feedback, and communicate about the request you sent. We do not use the forms to create a client account or a client database. Form submissions are delivered to our business email through our email-delivery provider; they are not intentionally stored in a website database.

Service providers and security

The website is hosted by Cloudflare Pages. Form messages are transmitted through Cloudflare Pages Functions and the Resend email-delivery service to our business inbox. Those providers may process technical data as needed to operate, protect, and deliver their services under their own terms and privacy policies. We use server-side configuration for service credentials and apply reasonable technical safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Retention

Messages may remain in our email inbox and provider logs for as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain business records, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations. To request deletion of a message we control, contact us at the address below. Deletion from third-party backups or logs may depend on the provider's retention practices.

Your choices and contact

You can choose not to use the forms and instead email acsktech@gmail.com. Contact us there with privacy questions or a request concerning information you submitted. We will review requests subject to applicable law and legitimate recordkeeping requirements.

Changes to this policy

We may update this notice when our practices or legal obligations change. The latest update date will appear at the top of this page.
