The use case

One support journey, four capabilities

A reference scenario for how a Salesforce Service Agent can combine instructions, topic routing and configured actions. Actual behavior depends on the Salesforce org's setup, permissions and data.

🛡️

Customer verification

Follow the configured verification steps before providing protected, account-specific details. Never claim verification succeeded without a confirmed result.

🧾

Case management

Gather the issue summary and required details, use the configured case action, and report the case number only when the action confirms success.

🧭

Topic routing

Route customer intents to the relevant configured topic, including case support, verification and general FAQ assistance.

💬

General FAQ

Answer from approved instructions and knowledge. If information is unavailable or uncertain, clarify or route to a human support path.

🔐

Privacy boundaries

Do not disclose another customer's protected information or bypass verification. Use fictional data in demos and keep secrets out of public assets.

✅

Validation

Test normal requests, missing details, topic changes, failed actions and unauthorized requests in Preview before presenting outcomes as verified.
